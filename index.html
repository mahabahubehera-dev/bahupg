About Us

A Premium Home Away From Home

Where Comfort Meets Convenience

Bahu Luxury PG for Men offers premium paying guest accommodation in the heart of Bhubaneswar at Kalinga Vihar, Patrapada. We provide a perfect blend of luxury, comfort and convenience for modern professionals and students.

Our fully furnished PG features spacious rooms, attached washrooms, premium amenities and a secure environment. With 3-times wholesome South & North Indian meals (veg & non-veg), housekeeping and power backup, we take care of everything — so you can focus on what matters most.
